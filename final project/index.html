KEEP YOUR VEHICLE READY

Small checks.
Better journeys.

Use these general reminders as a starting point. Always follow your vehicle owner's manual and qualified technician guidance.

01

Check fluid levels

Review engine oil and other service fluids according to the owner's manual.

02

Inspect tyres

Check tyre pressure when cold and look for visible wear or damage.

03

Pay attention to brakes

Have unusual noises or changes in braking checked by a qualified mechanic.

QUICK REFERENCE

Maintenance checklist

  • Review the owner's manual service schedule.
  • Check tyre pressure and visible tyre condition.
  • Inspect lights, wipers, and windscreen condition.
  • Check fluid levels using the recommended procedure.
  • Book a qualified inspection if warning lights appear.

This general checklist is educational and does not replace vehicle-specific service guidance.
